## 1. Objective

Phase 4 establishes the deterministic agricultural evapotranspiration and water demand layer that converts continuous meteorological observations generated by Phase 3 (`simulation/weather.py`) into physical moisture loss rates:
$$\text{Meteorology } (T, RH, R_s, u_2, P) \longrightarrow ET_0 \longrightarrow K_c \longrightarrow ET_c \longrightarrow P_{\text{eff}} \longrightarrow D_{\text{crop}}$$

This engine provides the physical input signals for the downstream **Water Demand Fuzzy Inference System (FIS 3)** and **Main Irrigation FIS (FIS 4)** without resorting to empirical black-box regression or machine-learning surrogates.

---

## 2. FAO-56 Penman-Monteith Methodology

The reference evapotranspiration ($ET_0$) is computed strictly following the Food and Agriculture Organization Paper No. 56 (*FAO-56: Crop Evapotranspiration — Guidelines for Computing Crop Water Requirements*, Allen et al., 1998).

The FAO-56 Penman-Monteith model combines energy balance at the crop surface with aerodynamic mass transfer principles:
$$ET_0 = \frac{0.408 \Delta (R_n - G) + \gamma \frac{900}{T + 273} u_2 (e_s - e_a)}{\Delta + \gamma (1 + 0.34 u_2)}$$

The hypothetical reference crop corresponds to:
- An extensive surface of green grass of uniform height ($0.12\text{ m}$).
- Fixed bulk surface resistance $r_s = 70\text{ s/m}$.
- Fixed surface albedo coefficient $\alpha = 0.23$.
- Actively growing, completely shading the soil, and optimally watered without soil-moisture stress.

## 4. Unit Conversions and Temporal Scale Reconciliation

The simulation operates on a **1-minute discrete timestep** ($\Delta t = 1\text{ min} = 60\text{ s}$). The FAO-56 formulation requires consistent energy and mass fluxes:

1. **Instantaneous Power to Timestep Energy**:
   $$\text{Solar Energy } R_s [\text{MJ}/\text{m}^2] = R_s [\text{W}/\text{m}^2] \times 60\text{ s} \times 10^{-6} = R_s \times 6 \times 10^{-5}$$
2. **Sub-Daily Rate to Hourly Rate**:
   $$R_s [\text{MJ}/(\text{m}^2\cdot\text{hour})] = R_s [\text{W}/\text{m}^2] \times 3600\text{ s} \times 10^{-6} = R_s \times 0.0036$$
3. **Daily Equivalent Energy**:
   $$R_s [\text{MJ}/(\text{m}^2\cdot\text{day})] = R_s [\text{W}/\text{m}^2] \times 86400\text{ s} \times 10^{-6} = R_s \times 0.0864$$
4. **ET0 Depths and Rates**:
   - $ET_{0,\text{hr}} [\text{mm/hour}]$: Calculated via FAO-56 Eq. 53 with hourly coefficient $37$.
   - $ET_{0,\text{step}} [\text{mm/min}] = ET_{0,\text{hr}} / 60$: Actual water depth evaporated in 1 minute.
   - $ET_{0,\text{rate}} [\text{mm/day}] = ET_{0,\text{hr}} \times 24$: Instantaneous 24-hour rate equivalent.

## 5. Vapor-Pressure and Psychrometric Calculations

### 5.1 Barometric Pressure and Psychrometric Constant
- **Atmospheric Pressure** (FAO-56 Eq. 7):
  $$P = 101.3 \left(\frac{293 - 0.0065 \times 53}{293}\right)^{5.26} = 100.675\text{ kPa}$$
- **Psychrometric Constant** (FAO-56 Eq. 8):
  $$\gamma = 0.665 \times 10^{-3} \times 100.675 = 0.06695\text{ kPa}/^\circ\text{C}$$

### 5.2 Saturation and Actual Vapor Pressures
- **Saturation Vapor Pressure** $e_s(T)$ (FAO-56 Eq. 11):
  $$e_s(T) = 0.6108 \exp\left(\frac{17.27 T}{T + 237.3}\right)$$
  - At $20^\circ\text{C}$: $e_s = 2.338\text{ kPa}$
  - At $25^\circ\text{C}$: $e_s = 3.167\text{ kPa}$
  - At $35^\circ\text{C}$: $e_s = 5.623\text{ kPa}$
  - At $42^\circ\text{C}$: $e_s = 8.196\text{ kPa}$
- **Actual Vapor Pressure** $e_a$ (FAO-56 Eq. 17):
  $$e_a = e_s(T) \times \frac{RH}{100}$$
- **Vapor Pressure Deficit** ($VPD$):
  $$VPD = \max(0.0, e_s - e_a)$$
  VPD represents the drying power of the air. During the Heatwave scenario, peak daytime VPD reaches $6.12\text{ kPa}$.

### 5.3 Slope of Saturation Vapor Pressure Curve ($\Delta$)
Tangential derivative (FAO-56 Eq. 13):
$$\Delta = \frac{4098 \cdot e_s(T)}{(T + 237.3)^2}$$
- At $25^\circ\text{C}$: $\Delta = 0.1886\text{ kPa}/^\circ\text{C}$
- At $38^\circ\text{C}$: $\Delta = 0.3582\text{ kPa}/^\circ\text{C}$

## 6. Radiation and Surface Energy Balance Calculations

### 6.1 Net Shortwave Radiation ($R_{ns}$)
Reflectance for the hypothetical grass canopy with albedo $\alpha = 0.23$ (FAO-56 Eq. 38):
$$R_{ns} = (1 - 0.23) R_s = 0.77 R_s$$

### 6.2 Net Longwave Radiation ($R_{nl}$)
Stefan-Boltzmann longwave thermal radiation emitted upward minus atmospheric counter-radiation (FAO-56 Eq. 39 & 55):
$$R_{nl} = \sigma (T + 273.16)^4 (0.34 - 0.14\sqrt{e_a}) \left(1.35 \frac{R_s}{R_{so}} - 0.35\right)$$
Where $\sigma = 2.043 \times 10^{-10}\text{ MJ}/(\text{K}^4\cdot\text{m}^2\cdot\text{h})$.
Clear-sky radiation $R_{so} = (0.75 + 2 \times 10^{-5} z) R_a$, where $R_a$ is extraterrestrial radiation computed from orbital geometry (solar declination $\delta$ and hour angle $\omega$).

### 6.3 Net Radiation ($R_n$) and Soil Heat Flux ($G$)
$$R_n = R_{ns} - R_{nl}$$
- **Daylight**: $R_n > 0$, peaking at $\approx 650\text{ W/m}^2$ equivalent ($\approx 2.34\text{ MJ}/(\text{m}^2\cdot\text{h})$). Soil absorbs heat:
  $$G = 0.10 R_n$$
- **Nighttime**: $R_s = 0 \implies R_n = -R_{nl} < 0$ ($\approx -0.15\text{ MJ}/(\text{m}^2\cdot\text{h})$). Soil releases heat to the cooling atmosphere:
  $$G = 0.50 R_n$$
- **Daily Net**: Over 24 hours, soil heat flux integrates to near zero: $G_{\text{daily}} \approx 0.0$.

## 7. ET0 Formulation and Temporal Discretization

The sub-daily formulation (FAO-56 Eq. 53) is evaluated at every 1-minute epoch:
$$ET_{0,\text{hr}} = \frac{0.408 \Delta (R_{n,\text{hr}} - G_{\text{hr}}) + \gamma \frac{37}{T + 273} u_2 (e_s - e_a)}{\Delta + \gamma (1 + 0.34 u_2)}$$
Where $R_{n,\text{hr}} = R_n / (1/60)$ and $G_{\text{hr}} = G / (1/60)$.
The 1-minute water loss is then $ET_{0,\text{step}} = ET_{0,\text{hr}} / 60$.

## 8. Crop Coefficient ($K_c$) Methodology

Crop coefficients account for physiological differences in plant canopy architecture, stomatal conductance, height, and aerodynamic roughness compared to reference grass.

Data is extracted dynamically from `data/crop_database.csv`:
- **Initial Stage**: Constant $K_c = K_{c,\text{ini}}$
- **Development Stage**: Linear interpolation $K_c(p) = K_{c,\text{ini}} + p \cdot (K_{c,\text{mid}} - K_{c,\text{ini}})$
- **Mid-Season Stage**: Constant $K_c = K_{c,\text{mid}}$
- **Late-Season Stage**: Linear decline $K_c(p) = K_{c,\text{mid}} + p \cdot (K_{c,\text{end}} - K_{c,\text{mid}})$

```
      Kc ^
         |              /------------------\
   Kc_mid|             /   Mid-Season       \
         |            /                      \
         |           / Development            \ Late-Season
   Kc_ini|----------/                          \---------- Kc_end
         |  Initial
         +----------------------------------------------------> Time
```

## 9. Crop Evapotranspiration ($ET_c$) Methodology

Under standard, non-stress agronomic conditions (FAO-56 Eq. 56):
$$ET_c(t) = K_c \times ET_0(t)$$

Because each zone has a distinct crop and growth stage, $ET_c$ varies across zones even under identical atmospheric conditions.

---

## 10. Effective Rainfall ($P_{\text{eff}}$) Methodology

Raw precipitation $P$ cannot be assumed to be 100% available in the root zone due to canopy interception, rapid surface runoff, and deep percolation.

The engine implements the standard **USDA Soil Conservation Service (SCS)** hydrological method:
- **Daily Scale**:
  $$P_{\text{eff}} = \begin{cases}
  P \times \left( \frac{125 - 0.2 P}{125} \right) & \text{if } P \le 83.3\text{ mm/day} \\
  \frac{125}{3} + 0.1 P & \text{if } P > 83.3\text{ mm/day}
  \end{cases}$$
- **Sub-Daily Scale**: Small precipitation pulses ($P \le 0.2\text{ mm}$) are intercepted by the canopy and evaporate directly. For rain events exceeding interception, $80\%$ is retained as effective infiltration ($P_{\text{eff}} = 0.80 \times (P - 0.2)$).
- **Physical Invariant**: $0.0 \le P_{\text{eff}} \le P$ strictly enforced under all conditions.

---

## 11. Water Deficit Methodology and Conceptual Distinction

### 11.1 Atmospheric / Crop Water Deficit ($D_{\text{crop}}$)
$$D_{\text{crop}} = \max\left(0.0, ET_c - P_{\text{eff}}\right) \quad [\text{mm}]$$
This represents the atmospheric water demand unsatisfied by natural rainfall over the interval.

### 11.2 CRITICAL DISTINCTION: $D_{\text{crop}}$ vs. Soil Moisture Error $e(t)$

| Property | Crop Water Deficit $D_{\text{crop}}$ | Soil Moisture Error $e(t)$ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Formula** | $D_{\text{crop}} = \max(ET_c - P_{\text{eff}}, 0)$ | $e(t) = SM_{\text{target}} - SM(t)$ |
| **Physical Meaning**| Atmospheric & canopy evaporative demand deficit | Root-zone soil moisture state deviation |
| **Units** | Millimeters of water depth [mm] | Percentage [\%] or Volumetric fraction [$\text{m}^3/\text{m}^3$] |
| **Dynamics** | Driven by radiation, wind, VPD, and rain | Driven by soil water balance and irrigation pulses |
| **Consumer FIS** | **Water Demand FIS (FIS 3)** | **Main Irrigation FIS (FIS 4)** |

## 14. Hourly and Daily Aggregations

- **Diurnal Cycle**: Reference evapotranspiration displays a bell-shaped diurnal curve. Nighttime $ET_0$ rates are minimal ($0.2\text{--}0.5\text{ mm/day}$ equivalent rate, representing tiny nocturnal stomatal and aerodynamic losses). Peak midday rates reach $16.0\text{--}24.0\text{ mm/day}$ equivalent rate around 13:30 to 14:00.
- **Hourly Peak**: Under Normal conditions, the maximum 1-hour accumulated $ET_0$ is $0.78\text{ mm/hour}$. Under Heatwave conditions, it peaks at $1.25\text{ mm/hour}$.
- **Consistency**: The discrete sum of all 1,440 one-minute step depths ($\sum_{i=1}^{1440} ET_{0,\text{step}}$) equals the integrated 24-hour total ($6.533\text{ mm}$) exactly, validating temporal conservation without numerical drift.

## 16. Assumptions

1. **Hypothetical Reference Grass**: Assumes an extensive, uniform grass canopy with standard roughness length and zero advection effects from surrounding dry terrain.
2. **Standard Non-Stressed $ET_c$**: Evaluates potential crop evapotranspiration under non-stressed conditions. Water stress coefficients ($K_s \le 1.0$) will be introduced in the **Soil Stress FIS (FIS 1)** and closed-loop soil dynamics (Phase 5).
3. **Ahmedabad Agronomic Baseline**: Elevation $z = 53\text{ m}$, latitude $\phi = 23.02^\circ\text{N}$, start date June 1 (Day 152).
4. **Soil Heat Flux Zeroing**: Daily soil heat flux $G \approx 0$ for 24-hour cycles; sub-daily $G = 0.10 R_n$ (day) and $0.50 R_n$ (night).

---

## 17. Limitations

1. **Dual Crop Coefficient Method Not Used**: Phase 4 uses the FAO-56 *single* crop coefficient ($K_c$) rather than the dual coefficient ($K_{cb} + K_e$) which splits transpiration from soil evaporation. This is appropriate for 3rd-year instrumentation project requirements.
2. **Microclimate Advection**: Does not model local Oasis effects or sensible heat advection from adjacent uncultivated dry plots.
3. **Static Growth Stage within 24 Hours**: For short 24-hour runs, phenological stage is constant. For multi-week horizons, stage interpolation progresses monotonically.
